I am a PhD student at the Maxwell Institute for Mathematical Sciences in Edinburgh, studying theoretical physics as part of the AGQ CDT (Algebra, Geometry and Quantum Fields Centre for Doctoral Training). I study holography and CFTs in the context of energy correlators as part of my PhD, but in the past have looked at the Bulk phase shift in AdS/CFT and NNLO corrections to QCD event shapes in my past two masters.

My PhD
======
I am a part of the AGQ CDT, which is a collaboration between 3 universities in Scotland (Heriot-Watt University, University of Edinburgh and University of Glasgow). This means that as part of my PhD I am doing research along with credited activities, like currently a course on Vertex operators!

My research is supervised by Matthew Walters, in the field of Energy correlators. 


MSc at Trinity College Dublin

I did a master's in Quantum Fields, Strings and Gravity at Trinity College Dublin as my second masters, though first non-integrated one.
